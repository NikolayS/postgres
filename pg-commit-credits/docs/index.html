How this was counted
  • Source. PostgreSQL master, through 2026-10-29. That is 54,611 commits since Oct 2001.
  • Rankings and overview. These come from parsing Author, Co-authored-by, Reviewed-by, Reported-by, Tested-by and Suggested-by lines in commits since 2019. When a commit has no Author: line, the committer is counted as the author. Name variants are merged by shared email and by ignoring middle initials. Credits written as free text aren't counted here.
  • Individual timelines. The 25-year charts, the roles table and the ledger come from searching commit messages for each name, so free-text credits like "Patch by X, reviewed by Y" are included. A line with only a name is counted as authorship, which was the convention before about 2015. Because of this, timeline totals can be slightly higher than ranking totals; for example, Nikolay has 25 commits for 2022–2026 in the timeline but 24 in the rankings.
  • Growth. Compares Jan 1–Oct 29, 2026 with the same dates in 2025. The share of commits that use these credit lines rose from 82% to 88%, which slightly inflates 2026 growth for everyone.
  • Committer. Anyone who committed to master during 2022–2026 (32 people).
  • Scripts and data. Every number on this page can be rebuilt with pg-commit-credits/run.sh on the claude/great-lovelace-gf5t4g branch of NikolayS/postgres.
